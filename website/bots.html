          Spending caps

          These can't be exceeded. When one is reached, test players carry on with their own routines until the hour or day rolls over. Usage is counted from what the API reports and kept across restarts. For a firm money limit, fill in your API's prices and a dollar cap.

          Players

          They get made-up player names and play survival like normal players: some solo, some in small teams. They make tools, set up bases away from spawn, mine, explore, go to the nether and trade. Real players can team up with them in game with /teamup <name>.
